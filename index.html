Space Mission: Learn Data Tables

Welcome, space explorer! On Planet Sweetopia, the aliens organize all their sweet treats using data tables. A data table helps them organize information in rows and columns.

Parts of a Sweetopian Data Table:

  • Headers: The titles at the top that tell us what's in each column
  • Rows: The lines that go across
  • Columns: The lines that go up and down

On Planet Sweetopia, data tables help aliens:

  • Sort sweet treats into groups
  • Count how many treats are in each group
  • Compare different groups of treats

Example: Favorite Desserts on Sweetopia

Commander Sprinkles asked 15 alien students about their favorite dessert. Here's what they said:

4 aliens liked cookies, 6 aliens liked ice cream, and 5 aliens liked cupcakes.

Dessert Tally Marks Number of Aliens

Total 15

Reading the Table:

We can answer questions using our table:

  • Which dessert did most aliens like? Ice Cream (6 aliens)
  • How many more aliens liked ice cream than cookies? 2 more aliens (6 - 4 = 2)
  • How many aliens were asked in total? 15 aliens
